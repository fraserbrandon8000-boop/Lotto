## Finding and evidence limits

**The result is compatible with ordinary random variation; no tested refinement justifies V2.** The primary was selected using the existing seeded no-edge policy, not by selecting the six highest marginal scores. Three hits therefore do not validate the ranking model. No next Lotto ticket was generated.

Full final ranks and tie-break order were not persisted. Scores reconstructed from frozen data/code and reconciled; tied intervals reported. F_structure and final no-edge selection have no number ranking. These are reproducible pre-draw **score-rank reconstructions**, not newly recovered saved ranks. Every reconstructed stored candidate score reconciled with the saved pre-draw score. #2339 was excluded from all fitting. G is a marginal proxy and omits the non-additive structure objective. E_pairs is flat after training correction; F_structure ranks combinations, not individual numbers; the final no-edge rule has no marginal ranking.

## Explicit answers

A. The table gives the pre-draw score ranks of 06, 23 and 28 under every applicable ranking. Original tie-break ranks are unavailable.

B. Top 6/8/10/12/15/20 winner counts are shown above, with tied boundaries preserved.

C. There is no corrected out-of-sample evidence that useful ranking information was systematically lost during ticket construction. Some larger pools captured more winners, as expected mechanically.

D. Three matches are compatible with normal variation; this single prospective observation does not establish a predictive signal.

E. No refinement passed the historical gate. Keep V1 frozen and gather fresh prospective evidence before promoting any challenger.
